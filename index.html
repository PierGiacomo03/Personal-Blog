Professional Experience

In-Company Consulting — Be. Shaping the Future
At Be. Shaping the Future in Milan, I designed an AI-powered ESG risk assessment tool for financial institutions, integrating NLP sentiment analysis (VADER/BERT) and ML models (XGBoost, ARIMA) to produce dynamic social risk scores.
